- M-side m_6_2_P_additive split of seg M a j1' at the cut c=j0N-a is now a
  verified `have fold: Br M' = P(seg M a (j0N-1)) @ ?Y` block (Finished PSS, 58s),
  using `leftmin` (this commit's predecessor 8b9ea44) for the left-minimality
  premise; `?Y = P(seg M j0N j1')` is hival from blk0fold.
- Several tactic landmines surfaced and were fixed:
  * `entry_seg`+`agree`+`entry_def` in one `simp` does not close `fst(M!x)=fst(N!x)`;
    establish the nth-equality `?X!j = N!(?a+j)` at list level first, then a single
    `simp add: entry_def` step.
  * `using c0 ac by linarith` on `?a + (?c-1) = ?j0N - 1` loops (the CLAUDE.md
    `?w`-style gotcha: `?c` re-expands the complex atoms `?j0N`/`?a` once `ac` is
    supplied); chain `c0`-only assoc then `ac` by `simp` via `also`/`finally`.
  * `thus ?thesis using <eq> by simp` to rewrite a seg endpoint is unreliable in
    this `?c`/`?a` context; switch to `arg_cong[OF eq, of "seg M ?a"]` for both
    segLOW and segHIGH (blk0fold's recipe).
- Remaining for B-J1>=1: Xdesc (LOW = take J1 (Br N'), descending_take) and
